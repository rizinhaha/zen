School Rules and Regulations

General School Rules
  • Students must enter and exit only through the designated school gate.
  • Arrive on time for the Flag Ceremony on Mondays and before classes on other weekdays.
  • Wear the proper school uniform and a valid ID from Monday to Friday.
  • Maintain personal discipline and respect teachers, staff, and fellow students.
  • Preserve school property and maintain campus cleanliness.
Dress Code Policy
  • Junior High School: White blouse and navy blue pleated skirt (girls), white polo and black/blue pants (boys).
  • Senior High School: White blouse and gray pleated skirt (girls), white polo with gray collar and gray/black pants (boys).
  • Wearing tight-fitting clothes, mini skirts, shorts, ripped jeans, and inappropriate attire is prohibited.
Offenses and Violations

Offenses Against Other Persons

  • Physical assault or fighting with another student or staff.
  • Use of disrespectful, vulgar, or indecent language or gestures.
  • Engaging in inappropriate public displays of affection (e.g., kissing, necking).

Offenses Against Property

  • Vandalism: Writing on walls, chairs, tables, or other school property.
  • Theft of school or personal property.
  • Forgery or falsification of school documents.

Offenses Against Public Morals

  • Possession or distribution of illegal drugs and alcohol.
  • Smoking cigarettes or vaping within school premises.
  • Engaging in gambling or betting activities.
Disciplinary Actions
  • First Offense: Warning.
  • Second Offense: Reprimand with community service.
  • Third Offense: Suspension.
  • Severe or repeated offenses may lead to expulsion.
Bullying Policy

Forms of Bullying

  • Physical: Hitting, pushing, kicking, or other acts of violence.
  • Verbal: Name-calling, teasing, or offensive remarks.
  • Psychological: Spreading false rumors, exclusion, or humiliation.
  • Sexual: Unwanted touching, sexual jokes, or harassment.
  • Cyberbullying: Online harassment, threats, or spreading false information.
